dedication

This is for my cousin Gerrit — for the Apple II he built himself, and let me play games on it when I was a preteen, for Joust and Barbarian, and for a pyramid game I never finished. I never said thank you at the time. I'm saying it now — and for The Mask of the Sun, that pyramid game which never let that kid get past the early rooms, yet inspired the creation of this interactive book. Thank you, this late.

And to the one who invented this very way of telling a story in the first place — words that wait for a reader's hand before they'll turn the page — a true genius, ahead of his time. This book, static words made interactive by a smart pre-computer system, only exists because that idea did first.
